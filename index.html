Preprint · Completed Research

Age-Based Performance Disparities in Machine Learning for Cardiovascular Disease Prediction

Investigated whether standard bias mitigation techniques can fix age-related fairness gaps in CVD prediction. A Random Forest classifier achieved 73.65% accuracy overall, but performance dropped from 86.47% for young patients to 69.91% for elderly patients. All four mitigation strategies failed, with some widening the gap to 18.57%, suggesting the disparity stems from structural complexity in geriatric data rather than correctable algorithmic bias.

68K Patients
